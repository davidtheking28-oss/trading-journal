Why It Works

Everything a spreadsheet can't show you

Broker, market, stock screener and portfolio — all in one place, no jumping between ten tabs

Your broker's report shows how much you made this month. It doesn't show which setups keep winning for you, when you're closing positions too early, or when you're over-exposed to one sector. ChartRoom takes every trade you make — stocks, crypto, any broker you connect — and computes real win rate, profit factor and average win/loss, so your next decision is based on what actually worked before, not a gut feeling.
