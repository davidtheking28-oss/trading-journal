This policy explains what data is collected when you use ChartRoom, how it is stored, and what your rights are. Your privacy matters to us — the Service does not sell data and does not use it for advertising.

1. Who is responsible for the data

ChartRoom is operated by an individual (not a registered company). For questions: davidtheking27@gmail.com

We do not collect location data, tracking cookies, or biometric information.

3. How the data is stored

All data is stored on Supabase (servers in the European Union — Frankfurt). Supabase implements Row-Level Security: only you can access your data. Communication is encrypted with TLS/HTTPS everywhere.

Broker API keys (IBKR, Bybit) are stored encrypted. We pass them only to import trades via a Supabase Edge Function — they are never stored on external servers.

5. Your rights

In accordance with Israeli privacy law and GDPR (to the extent applicable):

  • Access — you can export all your data from the app via Settings → Export Data
  • Deletion — send a request to davidtheking27@gmail.com and we will delete your account and all your data within 14 days
  • Correction — you can edit and delete trades directly in the app

6. Data retention

Active account data is retained for as long as the account is active. After a deletion request, data is deleted within 14 days.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for users aged 16 and under. We are not aware of any data collected from children. If you become aware of such a case — contact us for immediate deletion.

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use after an update constitutes agreement.
